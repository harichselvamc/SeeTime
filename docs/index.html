What it is

See Time is a small, focused Android app for comparing and converting time across any IANA time zone — built for people who work across time zones and are tired of mental math and stale converter websites.

Every offset, DST calculation, and conversion is computed on-device using the platform's own time zone database (java.time), stored locally with Room. There's no server, no account, no tracking, and no network permission at all — it works the same on a plane as it does at your desk.

Features

Offline-first

No internet permission, no network calls. Every time zone conversion runs entirely on your device.

DST-aware

Shows the real daylight-saving delta for each zone, not a guessed +1:00 — correct even for the handful of zones with unusual DST shifts.

Home screen widget

Pin your primary time pair to the home screen for an at-a-glance check.

Quick shortcuts

Long-press the app icon to jump straight to adding a time pair or reporting an issue.

12h / 24h format

Switch the clock format from Settings — applies everywhere instantly.

Material You

Dynamic color on Android 12+, plus a clean dark theme.
