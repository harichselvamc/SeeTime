Everything you need

Built for people working across time zones

No more mental math, no more stale converter websites — one small app that works instantly, everywhere.

Offline-first

No internet permission, no network calls. Every conversion runs entirely on your device.

DST-aware

Shows the real daylight-saving delta for each zone, not a guessed +1:00 — correct even for zones with unusual DST shifts.

Home screen widget

Pin your primary time pair to the home screen for an at-a-glance check.

Quick shortcuts

Long-press the app icon to jump straight to adding a time pair or reporting an issue.

12h / 24h format

Switch the clock format from Settings — applies everywhere instantly.

Material You

Dynamic color on Android 12+, plus a clean dark theme.
