Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

See Time ("the app") is developed and maintained by Harichselvam C ("we", "us"). This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your data when you use the app. The short version: we don't collect any of it.

1. Data we do not collect

See Time does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, because it has no mechanism to do so:

  • No account or sign-up of any kind.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No crash-reporting service that sends data off your device.
  • No INTERNET permission — the app cannot make a network request even if it wanted to. Every time zone offset and daylight-saving calculation is computed on-device using Android's built-in IANA time zone database.

2. Data stored locally on your device

The app stores the following, only in local app storage on your own device:

  • The time zone pairs you choose to add (for example, "Asia/Kolkata" → "Europe/London") and the order you arrange them in.
  • Your 12-hour / 24-hour display format preference.

This data never leaves your device through the app. We — the developer — have no access to it, no copy of it, and no way to see it.

3. Android system backup

See Time does not implement its own backup or sync. However, like most Android apps, it does not disable the operating system's standard app backup. If you have Android device backup enabled on your phone, your saved time zone pairs and format preference may be included in your device's backup to your own Google Account, the same way as any other app's local data. This is standard Android OS behavior tied to your Google Account, not something See Time itself transmits, accesses, or controls.

4. Permissions

See Time requests no runtime permissions (no location, contacts, storage, camera, microphone, or notifications) and declares no internet access in its manifest.

5. Children's privacy

See Time is a general-audience utility app and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from anyone at all.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional feature that does involve data — this page will be updated with a new "Last updated" date, and the change will be described here before it ships in an app update.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to harichselvamc@gmail.com, or filed as an issue on GitHub.
